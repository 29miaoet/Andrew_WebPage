Are you using a MacBook or iPhone to access Andrew's Website, using Safari browser? Unfortunately, the install button above will not work with Safari, however, you can follow the instructions below:

For mobile and iPad:
1. Open the homepage
2. Click the share button
3. Click "Add to home screen"

For desktop:
1. Open the homepage
2. Click "File"
3. Select the "Add to dock" option

Note: it is recommended to use another browser other than Safari to get the best results.
